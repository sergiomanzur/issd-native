# Independent stadiums and Mod Studio acceptance

The implementation adds independently authored pitch length, native background
maps, character artwork, palettes and stadium-local HD replacements. Logical
stadiums can share an original scenery template while retaining their own
geometry and artwork. The cartridge remains unchanged.

## Supported scope

Version 1 supports up to 32 logical slots and the eight original scenery
templates. Pitch origin and template width remain fixed. Length may be reduced
in 32-unit steps, down to 1536 engine units. Camera policy, goal aperture, net
depth and crossbar height retain the measured original constraints. This is
not arbitrary stadium architecture or a complete decompilation of the game.

Native character slots and palette spans are reserved explicitly. World maps
use the original page layout and at most 256 distinct metatiles per layer.
Some shortened template maps exceed that budget: the compiler rejects them
rather than truncating them. Authored final maps can fit the same geometry.

## Measured runtime evidence

Two same-template profiles completed uninterrupted matches through normal
controller inputs, with no RAM, score or clock modifications and no snapshot
continuations:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 1728 / 576 | Genuine scored goal | Observed | 50304 | 0–4 |
| 31 | 1664 / 576 | Genuine scored goal | Observed | 51689 | 0–2 |

The diagnostic input driver replaces only controller input and the bounded
probe termination/capture policy. Its build manifest hashes the production
objects. Results are in the private, ignored
`build/independent-stadium-validation/natural-full-matches/` directory.

Fresh ordinary menu selection of the distributable MEADOW and HARBOR examples
also verifies logical ID, base template, constructor dimensions, right goal
position, authored VRAM, palette and native world maps.

Native save/load checks cover unchanged artwork, changed palettes, deleted
uploads and complete artwork removal. Geometry identity enters save
compatibility; cosmetic identity triggers a deferred, one-time refresh.
Original static template resources are restored before applying remaining
custom resources. Refresh waits for original transfer and fade controllers.
Unchanged saves preserve loaded animation and pose history.

Controlled pre-construction snapshot fixtures passed all nine combinations of
snow/fine/rain and day/evening/night on the layout-0 custom profile. Original
initialization and fades produced live play with the requested weather/time,
correct geometry and unchanged owned native characters/palette. These fixtures
edit weather/time selection fields explicitly; they do not certify menu input
or every template's weather variant. Results are in `weather-fades/report.json`.

Replay acceptance uses genuine goals and covers native 8:7, 4:3, 16:10, 16:9
and 21:9 captures at native resolution and local HD 2×. The paused matrix
compares guest RAM and the native center exactly, and verifies that HD changes
the actual rendered output. Moving frames 400, 440 and 480 also passed the
same five-width, native/HD matrix on both profiles, including exact center
pixels, identical guest state and visible motion. Results are recorded in
`moving-replays.txt`.

## Original engine investigation

Fresh interpreter runs on all eight originals produced real scored replays.
`tools/ghidra/classify_stadium_trace.py` preserves raw ticks, PCs, generations
and queue fields in a measured dependency report. It distinguishes readiness
of the final measured transfer chunk from completion of a whole scene.
The observed chunk is delivered before subsequent streaming and scanline-1
rendering. Replay constructors and streamers retain the live upload generation.
Queued map updates generated by streaming may still exist at the PPU marker;
the preceding empty original DMA batch is recorded separately.

The historical strict certificate remains a separate schema. No token decoder
entry, arbitrary NMI completion or chunk boundary is relabeled as an invented
global decompression-complete event. Ghidra exports for the original loader
and DMA seams are retained privately under `build/ghidra-investigation/`.

The bounded geometry consumer audit surveyed 398 occurrences: 392 instruction
rows and six data rows. Of the instructions, 377 are ordinary derived-field
reads/writes and 15 are literal address occurrences. Followed literal flows
resolve to display-object records, bank-7F graphics offsets, tilemap attributes
and object-array bounds, rather than additional pitch geometry pointers.
Examples include `81D917→8CA95A`, `81DC75→86AA68`, `81EC1D→A4D3C5`,
`82F829→808E37` and `8BF6CC→85D807`. This closes the surveyed occurrences
and followed flows for fixed-origin, stock-width, shortened profiles; it is
not a proof about every possible indirect pointer in the ROM.

The original interpreter scenery harness executed **31,969,712 cases**, using
all **691 partition coordinates**, all eight callbacks and each supported
length. It checks low-ball pitch and goal-net envelopes with threshold
neighbors and interval representatives. Separate controlled original-code
tests exercise throw-in, corner/goal-kick routing, penalty, goal and crossbar
boundaries. These fixtures are distinguished from the natural full matches.

## Editor and artifacts

Mod Studio provides geometry and artwork tabs, drag editing, an initial-camera
overlay, compiled pixel previews, image import and local HD generation,
inline validation, undo/redo, cloning into unused slots, portable export and
isolated Test/Stop sessions. Invalid drafts disable launch. Test sessions own
their mod/config/save/log directories and stop only their child process.

The frozen editor is rebuilt with matching Tcl/Tk resources and checked away
from repository imports. Its authored-workflow selftest exercises invalid
drafts, compiled previews and portable export. Visible source-GUI Test/Stop
checks launched original ID 0 and custom IDs 8 and 31.

The distributable pack is `mods/independent_stadium_example/mod.json`.
MEADOW and HARBOR contain newly generated simple artwork, not extracted
cartridge pixels. They are authoring examples, not finished professional art.
The private ROM, snapshots, raw runtime dumps and extracted preview resources
are excluded from exports and public examples.

Windows, Linux and Android compile the shared implementation. Linux fresh-menu
startup passed on both custom profiles with verified geometry and logical IDs.
Android compilation covers arm64 and x86-64;
device gameplay has not been validated. The final full suite passed **421 tests,
with 5 skipped and 8 subtests passed**, in 3274.05 seconds. The nine new transfer
classification tests passed separately after that run's collection.
Results are retained in `build/independent-stadium-validation/full-suite-final.txt`.
The five skips were:

- Default-device audio timing requires an otherwise idle machine.
- The opt-in 35-match World Series acceptance was not enabled in this run.
- The legacy extra-stadium sustained natural-goal run was not enabled.
- Extended accelerated retail-period visuals were not enabled.
- No penalty-area snapshot fixture was available.

The independently recorded full custom matches and moving replay matrix above
are additional evidence; they do not silently replace or relabel these skips.
This work does not publish a 1.0 release.

Final independent review found two false-positive paths in the transfer report
validator, concerning scene identity and empty-batch ordering. Both were fixed;
nine focused retention/rejection tests passed, and the reviewer independently
reran them without remaining findings. Production cosmetic refresh/removal and
saved animation preservation had no further findings in that review.
